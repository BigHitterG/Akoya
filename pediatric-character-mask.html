Product design

Built around the clinical moment.

The open upper view supports forward and upward awareness, while the lower portion of the eyewear helps limit a child's direct view of activity below eye level.

Intended setting

For brief use by children ages 3 and older under continuous adult supervision in a clinical setting. Not a toy. Remove after the procedure and do not allow walking while worn.

Comfort and spacing

Soft foam supports create space between the face and printed eyewear, with an adjustable elastic cord helping maintain position.

Character options

Four friendly characters.

A small collection gives children a choice while keeping ordering and stocking straightforward for clinical teams.
