Akoya Eye Shield · Adult eyewear

A more comfortable view of adult care.

Help adults see less of the procedure. The Akoya Eye Shield helps limit the view of needles and activity below eye level while leaving an upper viewing area open for awareness and communication.

A soft, conformable foam interface and lightweight frame. Single-patient use. Nonsterile. Latex-free. Assembled in the USA.
