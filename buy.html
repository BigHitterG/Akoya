Akoya Eye Shield

Akoya Eye Shield combines an opaque lower region with a conformable interface designed to limit visibility of procedural activity below eye level while leaving an upper viewing area open. Actual obstruction depends on fit, positioning, facial anatomy, and gaze direction.

  • Nonsterile
  • Latex-free
