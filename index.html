What the design does

Limits the procedural view. Preserves an open upper view.

Akoya Eye Shield addresses one narrow source of discomfort: direct visual exposure to needles and procedural activity. Actual visual obstruction depends on fit, positioning, facial anatomy, and gaze direction.

Lower-field visual barrier

An opaque lower region and conformable interface are designed to limit visibility of activity occurring below eye level.

Open upper viewing area

The upper area remains open, allowing partial forward or upward visibility for awareness and communication.

Patent verification

Lower-field vision-obstruction technology described in an issued U.S. patent.

The technology and method described in U.S. Patent No. 12,629,282 B1 use an opaque lower region and a conformable interface to limit lower-field visibility while preserving partial forward or upward visibility. A patent documents intellectual property; it does not establish clinical effectiveness or regulatory clearance.
