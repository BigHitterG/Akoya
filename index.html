A shared Akoya design principle

Helps limit the lower view. Preserves an open upper view.

Both Akoya products use a physical lower-field visual barrier while leaving an upper viewing area open. Actual visual obstruction depends on fit, positioning, facial anatomy, and gaze direction.

Lower-field visual barrier

An opaque lower region is designed to help limit visibility of activity occurring below eye level.

Open upper viewing area

The upper area remains open, allowing partial forward or upward visibility for awareness and communication.
